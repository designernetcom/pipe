about Our Company

New Pipe Agencies is a reputable name in the plumbing industry, with roots tracing back to 1982 when Shri Ganpati Tube Corporation was founded by Shri Ramjiwan M. Mittal and Shri Shankarlal M. Mittal in Pune. In 1993, Mr. Rakesh R. Mittal joined the business and played a key role in its expansion, eventually establishing Pipe Agencies in 2001. After a few transitions, the firm was rebranded as New Pipe Agencies in 2009 under his sole proprietorship. Over the years, the business scaled from a 250 sq. ft. shop to an 10,000+ sq. ft. facility in Shelke Wasti, Gangadham, reflecting continuous growth and customer trust. We specialize in a complete range of plumbing materials and are proud distributors for Supreme Pipe Systems. Backed by our mother firm Shri Ganpati Tube Corporation and sister concern Shri Ganpati Enterprises, we continue to uphold our legacy with commitment, quality, and service excellence.
