About Us

New Pipe Agencies is a trusted name in the plumbing and piping materials industry, known for delivering quality products and dependable service. Our journey began in 1982 with the establishment of Shri Ganpati Tube Corporation by Shri Ramjiwan M. Mittal and Shri Shankarlal M. Mittal in Mangalwar Peth, Pune. With time, the business expanded, evolving into a legacy built on experience, trust, and commitment. In 1993, Mr. Rakesh R. Mittal joined the family business, bringing new energy and vision. To meet the growing demand and further strengthen our offerings, Pipe Agencies was launched in 2001 in partnership and later rebranded as New Pipe Agencies under the sole proprietorship of Mr. Rakesh R. Mittal in 2009. Over the years, we've grown from a modest 250 sq. ft. space to our current state-of-the-art 10,000+ sq. ft. facility at Shelke Wasti, Gangadham, Pune, reflecting our strong and steady growth. Today, we proudly offer a complete range of plumbing materials under one roof, serving as authorized distributors for Supreme Pipe Systems.
