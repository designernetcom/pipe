Overhead Water Tanks, Loft Tanks Septic Tanks

SILTANK offers premium overhead, loft, and septic water tanks made from 100% virgin, food-grade material, ensuring safe and hygienic water storage. Available in 100 to 10,000 litres with 2, 3, or 4-layer UV-stable, thermally insulated designs, they maintain water temperature while their ribbed structure adds strength and prevents bulging. Easy to install with lifting hooks and strong threaded covers, these BIS-compliant tanks ensure full capacity and durability. Unlike conventional concrete or Hume pipe septic tanks prone to leakage and corrosion, SILTANK septic tanks are maintenance-free, long-lasting, and environmentally friendly, providing a reliable, high-performance alternative with excellent value for money.

FEATURES AND BENEFITS

  • Rotationally moulded, one-piece construction - Septic tanks are manufactured by rotational moulding process which produces a one piece, seamless, watertight and strong product.
  • Unique design - Septic tanks have unique patented design which allows vertical settlement of the solids and are found to be much more efficient than conventionally built septic tanks.
  • Great strength - Robust design with unique rib structure provides superior structural integrity to the tank to meet various loading and handling requirements.
  • Simple and quick installation - These lightweight, ready-to-use underground water tanks offer quick installation, minimal excavation, and easy transport—ideal for cost-effective, durable solutions with minimal skilled labor.
  • Minimum space requiremen - Being available in ready to use form, these tanks can be installed in minimum space with minimum excavation
