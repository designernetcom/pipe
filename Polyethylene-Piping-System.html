Polyethylene Piping System

Polyethylene pipes are safe, durable, and cost-effective solutions for various applications such as potable water supply, irrigation, and bore wells. These HDPE pipes are made from virgin raw materials using advanced manufacturing technology. Strict testing of raw materials and finished products ensures compliance with national and international quality standards. Available in sizes from 20mm to 800mm and pressure ratings from PN 2.5 to PN 16, they come in PE 63, 80, and 100 grades. The pipes are manufactured in accordance with IS:4984 and ISO:4427 standards and are WRc-NSF, UK certified for potable water use.
